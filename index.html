01 · POWER SYSTEMSCompleted · Nov 2026

Protective Relay & SCADA Simulator

A distribution-feeder protection simulator that tells a temporary fault (reclose and restore power) from a permanent one (lock out), while only the breaker nearest the fault opens.

  • IEC 60255 and IEEE C37.112 inverse-time curves with an integrating timer, so trip time stays correct when fault current changes
  • Auto-reclose (79) and breaker-failure (50BF) logic, with a 0.80 s coordination margin between recloser and substation
  • SCADA operator console and a millisecond sequence-of-events log
  • 48 pytest tests checked against published curve values

02 · FPGA / DIGITAL DESIGNCompleted · Nov 2026

FPGA Serial Protocol Analyzer

A Verilog logic analyzer that listens to UART, SPI and I2C at the same time, decodes them in hardware, timestamps every event and freezes a capture when something goes wrong.

  • Decoders with two-flop synchronizers, glitch rejection, all four SPI modes and START/STOP skew protection
  • Block-RAM ring buffer with 6 trigger conditions (framing/parity error, I2C NACK, cut-off SPI word, data match)
  • Captures streamed over UART to a Python decoder
  • 29 cocotb + 9 host tests; DE10-Lite (MAX 10) board wrapper

03 · MONITORING & ALARMSCompleted · Nov 2026

Generator Monitoring & Alarm Dashboard

A model of a 500 kVA, 480 V standby generator with a control-panel alarm system that avoids nuisance trips the same way real generator controllers do.

  • Voltage, frequency, current and winding-temperature response to load steps (governor, AVR and thermal models)
  • Warning/critical alarms with hysteresis and on-delay timers, plus a time-stamped event log
  • Overload, load-rejection and motor-start scenarios with trend dashboards
  • 34 pytest tests, including a check that sensor noise can't make the alarms chatter

FPGA Vibration Monitoring System

A fixed-point Verilog pipeline that detects abnormal machine vibration: DC blocker → 4-band biquad filter bank → band energy → severity alarms → VGA diagnostic display.

  • The whole filter bank shares a single pipelined multiplier (one DSP block)
  • Alarms with hysteresis and persistence; RTL checked against a Python reference model with cocotb
